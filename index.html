Daily Nutrition Plan — Hard Gainer Protocol

72kg, 180cm, high metabolism, works 11 AM–7 PM, trains after work ~7:30 PM.

Daily Target: 2,800–3,200 kcal · 140–160g protein
Hit these numbers every day — including rest days. Muscle is built during recovery.
Meal 1 — Breakfast ~9:30 AM (At Home)
4 whole eggs scrambled in butter + 2 slices whole-wheat toast with peanut butter + 1 large glass of whole milk.
~750 kcal · 45g protein
Meal 1 — Breakfast (On The Go / Outside)
Foul medames wrap with tahini + boiled eggs from any street cart OR 2 cheese croissants + a large latte with whole milk.
~650-750 kcal · 30-40g protein
Meal 2 — Work Snacking 11 AM–7 PM (Graze All Day)
Keep at your desk: a bag of mixed nuts (almonds, cashews, walnuts), Greek yoghurt cups, protein bars, bagels with cream cheese, dried fruit + dark chocolate. Eat every 2-3 hours — don't wait until you're hungry.
~600-800 kcal · 30-40g protein
The Liquid Meal Shake (Daily — Pre or Post Workout)
400ml whole milk + 2 scoops whey protein + 2 tbsp peanut butter + 1 banana + 50g oats + 5g creatine. Blend and drink. Takes 2 minutes to make, impossible to feel full from.
~900 kcal · 60g protein
Meal 3 — Post-Workout Dinner ~9:00 PM (THE Most Important Meal)
Large portion of rice or pasta (250g cooked) + grilled chicken thighs, kofta, or shawarma (200-250g) + any vegetables you like + drizzle olive oil over everything for extra calories. Cook in bulk on weekends if possible.
~800-1000 kcal · 50-60g protein
Meal 3 — Quick Dinner Options (No Cooking)
Order a large chicken shawarma plate with extra rice + hummus, OR a double cheeseburger meal, OR a large mixed grill plate. When bulking, convenience > perfection. Getting the calories in matters more than eating clean.
~800-1000 kcal · 40-55g protein
Before Bed ~10:30 PM (Optional but Recommended)
A cup of Greek yoghurt with honey + a handful of nuts, OR a glass of whole milk with a scoop of casein/whey. Slow protein overnight.
~250-400 kcal · 20-30g protein
Daily Total: ~3,000-3,400 kcal · 150-170g protein
Hydration: 3+ litres of water daily. Add 500ml extra on training days. Creatine pulls water into muscle cells — if you are not hydrating enough, you will cramp and lose performance.
Your Split: 4-Day Upper/Lower Hypertrophy

Upper A (Chest & Back Power) → Legs (Full Lower Body) → Rest → Upper B (Shoulders & Arms) → Upper C (Chest & Back Hypertrophy) → Rest → Repeat. Never more than 2 days in a row. Legs once per week with full posterior chain. Face pulls every upper day for shoulder health. Each muscle hit with 10-14 sets per week across multiple sessions — the science-backed sweet spot for hypertrophy.

Progressive Overload Rules

Hit top of rep range on all sets → add weight next time.

Can't hit bottom of rep range → keep weight, fix form.

Add 2.5kg upper / 5kg lower body per progression.

If RPE 10 every set — you're overdoing it. Back off 5% and rebuild.

Creatine Protocol

Dose: 5g daily in your liquid shake — consistency beats timing.

Loading? Optional. 20g/day × 5–7 days then 5g. Or just start at 5g and saturate in ~4 weeks.

Hydration: +500ml water daily minimum. Creatine pulls water into muscle cells — if you are dehydrated you lose performance and cramp.

Rest days: Still take it. Every single day. No days off.
